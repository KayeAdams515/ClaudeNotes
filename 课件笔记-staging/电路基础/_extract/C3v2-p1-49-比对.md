## p5

- 该页讲了什么：电路的两种基本工作状态。

  - 稳态定义二（直流稳态）：`If only DC voltage/current source of is used in a circuit, and the circuit has been operating for a long time, then the current and voltages of any nodes in a circuit is tend to be stable, and won't change with time. This kind of circuit is called the DC steady-state circuit.`
  - 页末红字提问：`Q: What is an AC steady-state circuit?`
- 旧笔记里有没有？→ **有**（C3.1 §2.1 的 important callout 逐句对应，§2.1 末尾还专门做了 `> [!question] 课堂追问（课件 p.5 最后一行红字）`）
- 缺失或差异：
  1. 措辞差异（很小，但值得注意）：新版写 `the current and voltages of any nodes in a circuit **is tend to be** stable`。原句语法有误（应为 `tends to be`）；旧笔记按义译成「各处电流电压达到某个稳定值」，正确处理。
